Initial open-source release of AthenXiv

An open, multidisciplinary archive for research papers with OpenTimestamps
proofs, an in-browser PDF.js reader and a thirty-language interface, extracted
from the code running at https://athenxiv.com/.

Zero dependencies: plain PHP 8.1+ with a small MVC core, PDO for MySQL or
SQLite, and vanilla CSS/JS. The distribution contains no configuration, no
credentials, no uploaded files and no operational scripts - only the application
and its tests. Licensed MIT; the papers published on the live site keep their own
rights and licences.
